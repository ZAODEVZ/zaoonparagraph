# paragraph-review

zm.

queue a draft for the approval pipeline.

## what it does

pushes a draft into the review panel. the panel checks voice, checks facts, checks the lane, and either approves or kicks it back.

## checklist the panel runs

opens with "zm". all prose lowercase. no bullets, no numbered lists, no emojis, no em dashes. signs off with "BUILD ON" or "let's build" plus the ZABAL Team credit. sentences punchy and high-velocity. voice raw and authentic, not over-polished.

## output

on approve, the edition moves to publish and social cards get scheduled for peak times. on reject, it goes back to draft with notes.
