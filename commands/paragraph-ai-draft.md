# paragraph-ai-draft

zm.

generate a draft from a prompt or an outline.

## what it does

takes a prompt or an outline and generates an on-voice draft. paragraph ai does the writing, the voice rules in `VOICE.md` constrain it, and the 400+ edition voice-index keeps it aligned with the back catalog.

## input

a prompt, or a structured outline. name the lane: daily-3, deep-dive, or recap.

## output

a draft written to the matching folder under `content/`, opened with "zm", signed off with "BUILD ON" and the ZABAL Team credit, all prose lowercase, no lists, no emojis, no em dashes.

## next step

hand off to `paragraph-review`.
