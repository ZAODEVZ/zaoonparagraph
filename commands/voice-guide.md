# voice-guide

zm.

extract the formatting rules from the 400+ historical posts.

## what it does

reads the 400+ edition archive and pulls the recurring formatting and voice patterns out of it. lowercase prose, the "zm" open, the sign-off, sentence velocity, structure. reconciles them against `VOICE.md`.

## input

the archive under `research/voice-index`, ideally after `paragraph-migrate` has run.

## output

an updated read on the voice rules and any drift between the live rules and the actual back catalog. keeps `VOICE.md` honest against 400+ editions of real signal.
