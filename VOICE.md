this is the voice guide. every edition follows it. no exceptions. paragraph ai drafts to these rules and the review panel checks against them.

## rules

all prose is lowercase only. no caps for names, no uppercase anywhere in the body.

no bullet points. no numbered lists. no emojis. no em dashes.

open every post with the greeting "zm".

sign off with "BUILD ON" or "let's build" then credit the ZABAL Team.

keep sentences punchy and high-velocity. short, hard, fast.

prioritize raw authentic voice over polished prose. rough and real beats clean and dead.

## note on the sign-off

the two exceptions to lowercase are the sign-off "BUILD ON" and the "ZABAL Team" credit. everything else stays lowercase.

## how the rules get enforced

the voice-guide command extracts formatting rules from the 400+ historical posts and keeps this file honest. when the archive voice drifts, re-run voice-guide and reconcile.
