## to draft

Using VOICE-RECAP as the rulebook and the facts file plus the published posts as the only sources, draft a recap post about [subject]. Scoreboard line first. Sections with headers that belong to the subject, not to a template. Every mechanic explained in one plain line. The three readers addressed once near the end. Close on what is next, with the one real date, then subscribe, then the sign-off. Leave any unknown fact as a placeholder in square brackets and say who fills it. No emojis, no em dashes, no crypto vocabulary.

## to check a draft

Check this draft against VOICE-RECAP. List every sentence that breaks a hard rule, every phrase that sounds like a template, every fact not in the sources, and every place a mechanic is named without being explained. Then rewrite only those lines.

## to cut

Cut this draft to half its length without losing a single fact from the facts file or the scoreboard line.

## to make the socials

From this post, write the X post and the LinkedIn post. X: the scoreboard line, the winners, the link, the sign-off, under 280 characters. LinkedIn: three short paragraphs, what it was, the numbers, what is next, then the link. Same rules, same voice.
