# FACTS: ZABAL Gamez Season 1

Last updated: 3 September 2026, by IMan. Verified against `data/season-1-results.json` and `data/finals.json` on main of `zaodevz/zabalgames`, and The ZAO Newsletter Day 231 (19 Aug) and Day 236 (24 Aug). Paragraph and NotebookLM read this. They do not invent past it.

## The season

- The ZAO's three-month Build-A-Thon. Not a video-game contest. Free, online, open to anyone.
- June: workshops. July: open build. August: Finals.
- Three tracks: artist (music and visual), builder (software), creator (media and distribution). Nobody was assigned a track.
- 31 recorded workshop sessions in June, all transcribed at zabalgamez.com/recordings.
- 31 projects from 15 people in July.
- Six finalists, two per track, picked by Zaal after the board closed on 16 August. There was no vote.

## The Finals

- Head to head on WaveWarZ, live community battles, with a Space open for each.
- Scoring: the poll, the charts (live trading volume on the battle), a judges' panel. Best two of three.
- Artist final, 24 August, 5:00 PM EDT: n3m vs dee-13. One hour inside a Space, winner called before it ended.
- Creator final, 27 August, 5:00 PM EDT: uniquebeing404 vs presdency. Same shape. Judges: Thy Revolution, n3m, one seat unfilled.
- Builder final, 29 to 30 August: jdwalka vs ghostmintops. 24 hours, noon Saturday to noon Sunday EDT. Built in the open, tradeable throughout. Opening Space 11:00 to 12:00 Saturday, closing Space 12:00 to 1:00 Sunday. Judges: Thy Revolution, Iman Afrikah, paperhandpapi.

## Builds worth naming

- Proof Drop, ghostmintops. Fingerprints finished work locally, hands back a postable receipt. First July build on the site. zabalgamez.com/builds/proof-drop
- ZABAL Recording Scout, ghostmintops. Turns the recording archive into a builder-facing board.
- Chroma Poker, jdwalka. Poker inside Farcaster, live HUD, hand history across sites, real users.
- Ledger, dee-13. A manga: five art school friends, a talent for forgery, an underground operation. Read as a flipbook.
- ColorZAO, uniquebeing404. Paint a grey canvas to reveal an independent artist's work underneath.
- HOOD, presdency. A marketplace where people and AI agents both list and buy.
