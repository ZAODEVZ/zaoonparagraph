# zao newsletter

zm.

this repo is the scaffolding for the zao newsletter running on paragraph. voice rules, workflow, folder structure, and command definitions all live here so every edition ships in the same raw authentic voice.

## what's here

- `VOICE.md` — the voice rules. read this before writing anything.
- `WORKFLOW.md` — draft to review to publish to socials.
- `commands/` — the working commands: draft, review, migrate, voice-guide.
- `content/` — where editions live. three folders: daily-3, deep-dive, recap.
- `research/` — archive research and the 400+ edition voice-index.
- `socials/` — social cards dispatched per peak times after approval.
- `PARAGRAPH_RESEARCH.md` — background research on the paragraph platform.

## the loop

paragraph ai drafts, the review panel approves, we publish, socials go out. archive research pulls from the 400+ edition voice-index. emails and socials drop to clipboard after each edition.
