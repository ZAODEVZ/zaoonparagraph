# Paragraph: History & Features Research

> A basic research overview of **Paragraph** (paragraph.com, formerly paragraph.xyz) — a web3-native
> publishing and newsletter platform. Compiled July 2026.

## Overview

Paragraph is a publishing platform that lets writers run a website, newsletter, subscriber list, and
(more recently) an AI agent from a single place. It began life as a "web3-native" alternative to
Substack, combining traditional email newsletters with blockchain-based tools for distribution,
ownership, and monetization. Over time it has broadened into a more general-purpose publishing tool
while keeping its onchain roots.

- **Founded:** October 2021
- **Founder / CEO:** Colin Armstrong
- **Headquarters:** San Francisco, California
- **Original name:** "Papyrus" — renamed to Paragraph to better reflect the goal of open expression
  and conversation.
- **Total funding raised:** ~$6.7M

## Core Features

### Publishing & Newsletters
- Website + blog + email newsletter from a single account (a Substack-style workflow).
- Post customization and newsletter branding.
- Email automation to re-engage subscribers.
- Multiple contributors / co-authors on a single post.

### Web3 & Onchain
- **Collectible posts (NFTs):** readers can collect (mint) content as NFTs that can be bought, sold,
  and traded, creating a new revenue stream for writers.
- **Gasless NFT minting** so readers don't need to pay transaction fees to collect.
- **Permanent storage** via the Arweave decentralized storage network (DSN).
- **Token / NFT memberships:** build a community by selling NFT or ERC-20 token memberships instead
  of (or alongside) traditional paid subscriptions, giving readers a form of ownership.
- **Revenue splitting:** proceeds from collected content can be routed to a separate wallet or smart
  contract, enabling splits between co-authors and collaborators.

### Social & Distribution
- **Farcaster integration:** discussions from Farcaster are displayed automatically on posts; when a
  writer connects a wallet tied to a Farcaster account, Paragraph permissionlessly pulls in followers
  for one-click subscription.
- Onchain social features and messaging integrations for direct audience engagement.

### AI & Automation (newer)
- AI writing assistance (originally powered by GPT-4) that generates sentences and paragraphs based
  on the writer's previous work.
- A creator "agent" that helps with research, social posts, websites, SEO, and recurring workflows.

## Monetization Model
- Core publishing features are **free** for writers.
- Paragraph takes a **5% fee on primary sales** when readers collect (mint) content.
- Additional revenue for creators comes from token/NFT memberships and collectible posts rather than
  relying solely on paid email subscriptions.

## The Mirror Acquisition (2024)

Mirror was one of the earliest and best-known web3 publishing platforms. In May 2024, alongside its
$5M raise, Paragraph acquired Mirror and consolidated the two into a single onchain publishing stack.
Key points:

- Mirror blogs, posts, and subscriber data migrate to Paragraph **automatically**, requiring no action
  from creators.
- The stated goal was a **unified model for onchain publishing** and a streamlined product roadmap.
- Mirror pivoted its remaining team toward a social app ("Kiosk"); founder **Denis Nazarov** stayed on
  as an advisor through the transition.
- The deal — backed by Coinbase and Base — was widely framed as a consolidation of the web3 publishing
  space.

## Positioning

Paragraph competes with traditional newsletter/blogging platforms such as **Substack**, differentiating
through onchain ownership, collectibles, and token-gated communities. Its trajectory has moved from
"web3 Substack" toward a broader publishing platform that layers AI tooling and onchain monetization on
top of a familiar newsletter experience.
