# workflow

zm.

this is the pipeline every edition moves through. draft, review, publish, socials.

## the pipeline

paragraph ai handles drafting from a prompt or an outline. the review panel approves. we publish to paragraph. socials dispatch after.

archive research uses the 400+ edition voice-index so new drafts stay on-voice with the back catalog.

emails and socials go out as clipboard after each edition. copy, paste, ship.

social cards dispatch automatically per peak times once the edition is approved.

## the three content lanes

daily-3 is the high-frequency low-friction lane. quick updates, low ceremony, fast turnaround. lives in `content/daily-3`.

deep-dive is polished long-form analysis. the heavier lane. lives in `content/deep-dive`.

recap is weekly and monthly synthesis. pulls the threads together. lives in `content/recap`.

## from spec to ship

start with a prompt or outline. run paragraph-ai-draft. queue it with paragraph-review. once the panel approves, publish. social cards fire on peak times. clipboard the email and socials.
