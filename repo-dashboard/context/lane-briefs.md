# lane briefs

per-lane brief the repo hands to paragraph. one section per lane. the context command pulls the matching section into the pack.

## daily-3

- audience: builders scanning fast
- length: 300 to 600 characters
- shape: zm on its own line, one to two sentence fragments, one update or one build
- anchors: buzz a shipping line, data a streak or count, callout a single link as a card

## deep-dive

- audience: technical readers who want the why
- length: 300 to 800 words
- shape: lead straight into the friction point, sentence casing allowed, inline code where it helps
- anchors: buzz the telemetry, data the measured result, callout the wins page as a card

## recap

- audience: the community catching up on the week
- length: 1200 to 1600 characters
- shape: zm open, short punchy blocks, three build card placeholders, quiz as the final section
- anchors: buzz the week label, data submissions and builds, callout the build quiz as a rich card
