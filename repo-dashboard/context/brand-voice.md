# brand voice (extracted)

paragraph extracted this from the published logs and recent tweets, then reported it could not save it to its own memory (updateWorkingMemory tool missing). so the repo holds it. this is the descriptive guide. the enforced gate is voice/profile.json. where they differ, see the flag at the bottom.

## brandVoice (long-form, newsletter)

- tone: plain, direct, zero-bullshit developer-log. quiet confidence of compiled onchain execution. no corporate polish, no forced hype, no emotional decoration. hard realities: working code, empty leaderboards, verifiable links, open prs, raw metrics.
- rhythm: staccato short fragments, thoughts line by line with double carriage returns. parallel structures. clipped connecting clauses.
- casing: raw lowercase for daily and structured updates.
- commas: stripped within raw lines, reserved only for complex listings or long paragraphs (note: our gate is stricter, zero commas).
- openers: signature greeting on its own line, zm or zm. then straight into the timeline (year of the zabal day 189, day three of zabal gamez july).
- catchphrases: play a game take a board, it reads the record not your word, builders building for builders, the quiet work compounds, small moves out loud every day, the build is the application.
- closings: direct cta or repo link (start your run zabalgamez.com/quest) then the signature.
- signature variants seen: "- BetterCallZaal on behalf of the ZABAL Team" and "BetterCallZaal from the ZAO Team".

## socialVoice (short-form, x)

- tone: casual, community-first, web3 and farcaster dev audience. high energy, direct.
- casing: all lowercase, minimal punctuation.
- hooks: seed with zm (zm going live and coding with, zm we live in desktop spaces, zm my journal from last year shows).
- references: dev spaces, live-coding sessions with collaborators, journal callbacks.

## conflict flag vs the enforced gate

- commas: gate = zero everywhere. extracted guide = allowed in long paragraphs. your call which governs deep-dive long paragraphs.
- signature: gate enforces one exact line. extracted guide shows two variants. pick the canonical one or i allow both.
- greeting case: live posts use "zm" lowercase, guide writes "ZM". gate stays lowercase.
