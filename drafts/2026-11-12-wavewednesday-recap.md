WaveWednesday: Team AI is up 2-1 and a Christmas song started it

Three battle nights, one main event, 14 quick battles, 11 winners, three ties, and about $135 put behind songs by people who were sure. Team AI 2, Team HumanZ 1, one tie, 60 days left to settle it. That is the scoreboard. This is the week.

How a night works, in one line

Two songs go up in the room. You back the one you think wins, and the price moves as people pile in. Three signals decide it: the poll, the chart (who the crowd backed with money), and DJ Wavy, our resident ear. Best two of three. The artists get paid straight from the action, not from a sponsor.

Wednesday 4 November: the vibe sesh

We opened the week with a confession. The battle analytics had gone down the night before because the cache came off the leaderboards and the app tried to pull 172 battles at once. CPU to the ceiling, database locked, a couple of recent battles missing for a bit. Fixed by the time the room opened. Then a side quest on Google Antigravity, the new coding tool we are using to rebuild the home page section by section, and two tips from the room that every builder should have tattooed somewhere: never push to main before you test, and when it breaks, go back to the last commit that worked.
